What Pro unlocks is one thing: forced HiDPI. If your 4K display's
text looks softer than the built-in screen, that is the setting
that fixes it — card ⋯ menu → Force HiDPI on. It writes a display
override and needs a restart, and macOS will ask for your
administrator password to allow the write. The app never sees that
password.

Two things worth knowing before you explore:

  · Scroll on the menu bar icon to change the brightness of
    whichever display your pointer is on, without opening anything.
  · DDC power-off and input switching cannot be undone from
    software on some monitors — both ask first, and both say so.
